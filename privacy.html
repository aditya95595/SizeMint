PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy, without the vague bits.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit SizeMint or use its image-compression tools. The short version is simple: the core compressor is designed to process your image files in your browser rather than uploading them to a SizeMint image server.

1. Images you choose to compress

When you select an image, your browser reads the file and SizeMint uses browser APIs such as image decoding, canvas encoding and local object URLs to create a compressed result. The normal compression workflow does not send the image contents to a SizeMint application server, and SizeMint does not maintain a server-side library of your compressed or original images.

Files can remain available to the browser for the current page session until you clear the tool or close the page. The browser and your operating system remain responsible for local copies, caches and other device-level storage.

2. Information needed to deliver the website

SizeMint is hosted as a static website. The hosting and security infrastructure may process ordinary technical request information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as IP address or network information, requested URL, timestamps, browser or device characteristics, referrer information and security signals. This infrastructure-level processing is separate from the compressor's image-processing workflow.

SizeMint does not operate an account system, image database, advertising profile database or application analytics database in the current version of the site.

3. Advertising and Google services

SizeMint may use Google AdSense or another advertising provider to help support the site. When advertising is enabled, the advertising provider may collect or receive information associated with ad delivery and measurement. Depending on the service and your settings, this can include cookies or similar identifiers, IP address, browser or device information, approximate location derived from network information, and information about interactions with ads.

Third parties, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser, or use web beacons, IP addresses and similar technologies, as a result of serving advertisements on the site. These technologies can be used to deliver, measure, limit or personalize advertising, subject to the provider's policies and your available choices.

For information about Google's handling of information on partner sites, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Google's advertising policies and privacy controls may change, so this link is the authoritative source for Google's current practices.

4. Personalized advertising and consent

Where applicable, SizeMint will use the consent and privacy mechanisms required by the advertising provider for visitors in jurisdictions where consent is required for personalized advertising or the use of advertising cookies. In particular, Google requires publishers serving personalized ads to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland to use an approved consent mechanism for the applicable traffic.

If you do not consent to a particular optional advertising technology, advertising functionality may be limited or non-personalized. You can also use the privacy and cookie controls provided by your browser and, where available, the advertising provider.

5. Cookies

The image compressor itself does not require cookies to select, compress or download images. Advertising and other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies when those services are enabled. A cookie can be stored or read by the relevant third party under its own policies and settings.

6. Third-party links

SizeMint may link to third-party services from time to time. Opening an external website takes you outside SizeMint and subjects you to that service's own privacy notice, terms and technical practices.

7. Children's privacy

SizeMint is a general-purpose utility. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children, and the site does not provide an account-registration system.

8. Data security

SizeMint uses browser-side processing for its core image tool and applies standard web security controls to the site. No website or device can guarantee absolute security, so you should use a trusted browser and device and avoid uploading or processing information on a website when you are not comfortable with the surrounding environment.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site's functionality, hosting, advertising or third-party services change. The date at the top of this page identifies the current version. If a change materially affects how information is handled, the published policy will be updated before or when the relevant functionality is introduced where appropriate.

10. Questions and support

For privacy questions or requests about the site, use the information provided on this website. Please do not submit private, financial, identity or other sensitive information through an unsecured public channel.

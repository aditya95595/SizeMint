PRIVACY POLICY

Your images stay in your browser.

Last updated: September 27, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how SizeMint handles information when you use the website and its browser-based image compressor.

Image files and local processing

SizeMint's core compressor is designed to process selected images locally in your browser. The application does not require an image-upload endpoint or server-side image storage. During normal compression, your selected image contents are read by browser APIs, encoded locally and turned into downloadable files.

Because the compressor is client-side, SizeMint does not intentionally receive the contents of the images you select for compression. You should still avoid processing confidential material on any website unless you are comfortable with the risks of the device and browser environment you are using.

Information handled by hosting infrastructure

Like other websites, SizeMint is delivered through web hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as network information, request details, browser information and security-related signals. SizeMint does not use an application database for compressor files.

Advertising

SizeMint may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to support the website. When advertising is enabled, advertising providers may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, device information or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize ads, subject to their own policies and the choices available to users.

Google's disclosure about how it uses information from sites and apps that use its services is available through How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Google requires publishers to provide disclosures about third-party cookies and related technologies used for ad serving. If SizeMint enables personalized advertising for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the site will use the consent mechanism required by Google for that traffic before requesting personalized advertising.

Cookies and similar technologies

The compressor itself does not need cookies to process images. Advertising or other optional third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies when they are enabled. Your browser may provide controls for blocking or deleting cookies, although blocking some technologies can affect advertising functionality.

Third-party links and services

SizeMint may link to third-party services such as GitHub for project support. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. SizeMint is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Children

SizeMint is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This notice may be updated when the service, hosting, analytics or advertising arrangements change. The current version published on this page is the version that applies to website use at the time you visit.

Contact and support

For bug reports or questions about the project, use the SizeMint issue tracker.

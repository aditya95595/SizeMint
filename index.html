How to compress an image

  1. Select one or more JPG, PNG or WebP images.
  2. Choose a target such as 100KB, 200KB, 500KB or 1MB.
  3. Optionally set an output format or maximum width.
  4. Click Compress images and download the results.

Images are processed in your browser. The site does not need to upload your photos to a processing server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I compress an image to exactly 100KB?

The compressor aims for the selected target and gets as close as practical. Exact byte-for-byte sizes are not always possible because image encoders work in discrete quality and dimension settings.

What target sizes are supported?

Presets range from 20KB to 5MB, including 50KB, 75KB, 100KB, 150KB, 200KB, 250KB, 300KB, 400KB, 500KB, 750KB, 1MB, 1.5MB, 2MB and 3MB. You can also enter a custom target.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Compression is performed locally in your browser by JavaScript. Your selected files are not sent to this website's server by the compressor.

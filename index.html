IMAGE COMPRESSION, EXPLAINED

Why compress to a target size?

Many upload forms, profile systems and document portals impose a maximum file size. SizeMint takes a target-first approach and reports the real result.

JPG, WebP or PNG?

JPG

A broadly supported lossy format suited to photographs.

WebP

A modern web-friendly format with a useful size/quality balance.

PNG

Lossless and useful for transparency, screenshots and graphics.

AVIF input

SizeMint accepts AVIF input when the browser can decode it.

What happens inside your browser?

The compressor uses standard browser image APIs to decode, resize and encode candidate results. The resulting Blob is offered directly for download.

Privacy by design

The core compressor processes selected images in your browser. See the Privacy Policy for current disclosures.

FAQ

Questions people have before compressing an image

Does SizeMint upload my images?

No. The core compressor is designed to process selected images in your browser.

Can SizeMint guarantee an exact file size?

No. File size depends on image content, dimensions, browser encoders and format.

What happens if the target is too small?

The compressor tries progressively smaller dimensions and encoder settings and reports best effort when necessary.

Why can PNG be difficult to shrink?

PNG is lossless and its quality parameter does not work like JPEG/WebP quality.

Can I compress several images at once?

Yes. Select multiple supported images and process them individually in your browser.

Can I reduce image dimensions?

Yes. Set a maximum width, and the compressor may also reduce dimensions when needed.

How it works

Drag, preset, download

Draw a rectangle directly on your image — outside the box dims so you can see exactly what stays. Pick a preset like 1:1 or 16:9 to lock the shape while you drag, or leave it on Free for any shape. Dragging inside an existing box moves it.

Full-resolution cuts

The preview is scaled to fit your screen, but the download is cut from the original at full resolution — a 4000-pixel photo gives you a 4000-pixel-wide crop region, not the small preview.

Private by design

Cropping uses local canvas APIs only. Your images never leave your device — see the Privacy Policy for the current disclosures.

FAQ

Questions about cropping images

How do I crop an image to a square?

Choose the 1:1 preset, then drag on the image to draw a square region. Drag inside the box to reposition it before downloading.

What formats can I download the cropped image in?

PNG for lossless quality, or JPG for smaller files. JPG fills transparent areas with white.

Is there a minimum crop size?

Yes — 16×16 pixels. Smaller selections are rejected with a notice.

Is cropping done locally or uploaded?

Entirely locally. The image is drawn to a canvas in your browser and the crop is cut from the full-resolution original.
